I'm an FPGA design engineer in the Soft IP team at [NanoXplore](https://nanoxplore.com), where I develop IP cores for the company's space-grade FPGAs (NG-MEDIUM, NG-ULTRA and ULTRA300). My current IP handles clock generation, built around the PLL and WFG primitives, and is designed, verified and validated under the ECSS flow.

Before that, during my research internship at NanoXplore, I benchmarked high-level synthesis tools (Vitis HLS, Bambu, Catapult) to see how HLS could fit into the NanoXplore design flow, and ported a Galaga arcade design to the ULTRA300, cabinet included. At the Laboratoire d'Astrophysique de Bordeaux, I wrote VHDL signal-processing modules on Intel Agilex 7 for the ALMA radio telescope's Wideband Sensitivity Upgrade. I earned my engineering degree in electronics, majoring in embedded systems, at ENSEIRB-MATMECA (Bordeaux INP).

**I'm looking for a PhD in FPGA-based systems for space and astronomy.** If you have a position or a project in that area, I'd be glad to hear from you. My full background is on the [resume](/cv/) page.

Outside work, I'm into astronomy, ham radio (CEPT/HAREC) and DIY electronics. I also led the rocketry division of EirSpace, a student aerospace association, building flight avionics for the mini-rocket teams at C'Space.
